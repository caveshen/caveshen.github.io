# The Interview

The portfolio site's domain language. The site is a playable scene: a
visitor approaches a character and holds a dialogue with them.

## Language

**Badger head**:
The character's portrait bust, the site's mark.
_Avoid_: avatar, logo, icon art

**Approach light**:
The soft edge-light that gathers around a character who can be
approached. It invites, then stands down once the visitor engages.
_Avoid_: rim glow, outline, halo, orb, approach glow

**Approach prompt**:
The stage question that drifts into view when a visitor considers a
character. Activating it starts the dialogue.
_Avoid_: approach button, chip, tooltip, floaty text, approach hint

**Glass**:
The site's shared translucent material. Surfaces made of it show the
scene through themselves.
_Avoid_: card background, panel style

**Plaque**:
The glass surface that carries a dialogue.
_Avoid_: card, dialogue box

**Stage voice**:
The narrator's register — italic, lowercase, murmured stage
directions.
_Avoid_: caption style, hint text
